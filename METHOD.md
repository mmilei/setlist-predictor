# Probable setlists: Gorillaz and The Strokes at Primavera Sound Buenos Aires 2026

> Generated by `analyze.py` (this header comes from `METHOD.md`). Data downloaded on 2026-10-10.

**The festival:** Club Ciudad de Buenos Aires (Núñez). **Saturday 11/28: Gorillaz**; **Sunday 11/29: The Strokes**.
As of 2026-10-10 there is no official schedule or set length ([La Nación][ln], [Indie Hoy][ih]), so the set length is
the median, in songs, of the festivals each band played on this tour.

## Method

- **Source:** the official [setlist.fm](https://www.setlist.fm) API, with every show of the current tour and no
  sampling. Each show keeps its URL (`shows.source_url`) and raw responses are cached in `raw/`. No need to add the
  previous tour: the current ones have 30 and 47 shows with a setlist.
- **Festival or headline show:** the API doesn't flag it. The list lives in `build_db.py` (`FESTIVALS`), checked by
  hand on the web.
- **Tapes** (recorded intros and outros): kept in the database with `is_tape = 1`, but they don't count as songs.
- **Probability = current repertoire × festival factor**, with a model specific to each band (`BANDS` in
  `analyze.py`):
  - **The tour's real legs.** Gorillaz: UK and Ireland (Mar-Apr) · Europe, summer festivals (Jun-Aug) ·
    North America (9/17-10/8). The Strokes: US summer (Jun-Jul) · Japan and West Coast (Aug) · US Southeast
    (9/12-9/20) · Queens and Europe (from 10/2).
  - **Current repertoire:** frequency weighted by leg. The **latest stretch** (its last 10 shows at most) weighs 4: for
    Gorillaz, the 10 shows since Charlotte (9/23), when the set locked in after Shaky Knees; for The Strokes, the 3
    shows since Queens (10/2), where the repertoire changed. The rest of the current leg weighs 1, the previous leg
    0.5 for Gorillaz and 0.75 for The Strokes (higher because their latest stretch has only 3 shows), and the rest 0.1.
  - **Festival factor:** Buenos Aires is a festival (~1.5 h), so what matters is which songs survive the cut. Each
    festival is compared with the 4 closest headline shows of the same leg: a song that always plays at headline shows
    but gets dropped at festivals gets a low factor (for example, Rock the House and Garage Palace for Gorillaz, which
    didn't play at Shaky Knees). Festivals in the most recent leg with festivals weigh 1, the previous leg 0.5 and the
    rest 0.25; the factor is smoothed toward the band average and capped at 1.
  - **Occasional guests** (added 2026-10-11, Gorillaz only): if a song plays with an occasional guest in 75% or more
    of its appearances (`DEP_SHARE`), it is played because that guest was there (the leg's opening act or an artist on
    the same festival bill), and they won't be in Buenos Aires. Those appearances weigh 25% (`GUEST_WEIGHT`). For
    example, Garage Palace with Little Simz, Rock the House with Del the Funky Homosapien or The God of Lying with Joe
    Talbot. Guests who join in 75% or more of the shows are part of the touring band (Bootie Brown, Kara Jackson,
    Posdnuos) and don't discount. Neither does Trueno, who is from Buenos Aires (`local_guests`), and The Manifesto
    goes into the playlist by hand (`must`). No artist on either day's lineup has a recorded song with Gorillaz or
    The Strokes (checked on 2026-10-11).
  - The set length is the median of the festivals in the last two legs that had festivals.
  - In the appendix, per-song statistics are unweighted.
- **Probable setlist:** the N most probable songs, with N equal to the median festival length. The order comes from
  comparing every pair of songs: A goes before B if, in the shows where both play, A comes first with more weight (the
  repertoire weight, so the latest stretch rules), and each song is placed by the share of those comparisons it wins.
  Then the fixed transitions of the latest stretch are glued in: if B follows A in 75% or more of the cases, B goes
  right after A.
- **Playlist:** no length cap ("I want to hear everything I'm quite likely to hear"). It takes every song with at least
  15% probability (`MIN_P`), every song in 50% or more of the current repertoire even if festivals usually cut it
  (`MIN_REP`), and the probable setlist, each in its typical position. The real show will be ~18 songs in ~1.5 h.
- **Filler** (added 2026-10-11): songs from each band's filler albums (`extra_albums`: for Gorillaz, Demon Days and
  Plastic Beach, not the debut; for The Strokes, their first 3) that played in the last two legs, which are the ones
  rotating in the set's free slots today, most probable first, with a 150-minute ceiling (`TARGET_MIN`). Each one goes
  into its typical relative position, before the closing block and never in the middle of a fixed transition. They're
  marked "(filler)".
- **Section:** `encore` if the song is in the encore in more than half of its appearances; otherwise `opening`,
  `middle` or `closing` by relative position (< 0.15, middle, > 0.8).
- **Rotate in the same slot:** songs with a similar mean position (±0.12) that almost never play in the same show.

## Reproduce

```
python build_db.py   # needs SETLISTFM_API_KEY in the environment or in .env; uses the raw/ cache (--refresh to re-download)
python analyze.py    # regenerates RESULTS.md and playlists.csv
```
